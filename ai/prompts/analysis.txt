{job_description}

APPLICATION DEADLINE:
{deadline}

Perform the following analysis:

1. Determine the overall CV-to-job alignment score from 0 to 100.

2. Identify the most important job requirements that are clearly supported by the CV.

3. For each matched requirement, provide concise evidence from the CV.

4. Identify important job requirements that are not demonstrated in the CV.

5. Identify requirements where the CV provides partial or weak evidence.

6. If an application deadline is provided:
   - determine how much time remains;
   - evaluate which missing or weak skills could realistically be improved before the deadline;
   - include ONLY realistically achievable improvements in the roadmap;
   - do not include skills that require unrealistic amounts of time to develop.

7. Provide truthful recommendations for improving the CV using the candidate's existing experience.

8. Generate a tailored cover letter based only on verified information from the CV and job description.

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent experience.
Do not assume a skill exists without evidence.
Do not treat the score as hiring probability.
Do not recommend unrealistic skill improvements.
